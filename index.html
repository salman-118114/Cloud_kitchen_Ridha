The Malabar table

Soft pathiri, thick orotti, slow-cooked curries and tea-time snacks — the everyday food of Kozhikode, cooked for your order.

  • അരിപ്പത്തിരി

    Ari Pathiri

    Paper-thin rice-flour bread, soft enough to soak up every bit of curry.

  • ഒറോട്ടി

    Orotti

    Thick rice roti with grated coconut, cooked on a clay pan. Best with beef or chicken.

  • കറികൾ

    Malabar curries

    Chicken curry, beef varattiyathu, fish curry and mutton stew — made for pathiri and orotti.

  • പലഹാരം

    Unnakkaya, pazham nirachathu, kallummakkaya and erachi pathiri, with a glass of sulaimani.

About Ridha Cloud

A Malabar kitchen, without the dining room

Kozhikode has cooked for traders from across the Arabian Sea for centuries. What came of it is Malabar food: kaima-rice biryani sealed and slow-cooked, rice pathiri rolled paper-thin, coconut, curry leaf and roasted spice.

Ridha Cloud is a cloud kitchen. There are no tables and no counter — only the kitchen. You order a day ahead, we cook for your order alone, and it comes to your home.

  • Cooked to order Nothing is made until you ask for it.
  • Delivered Free within 10 km of our kitchen.
  • Two or two hundred Family meals and party orders.
